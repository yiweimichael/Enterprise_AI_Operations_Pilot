# Operations Onboarding Checklist

## Week 1
- Get access to the CRM, the analytics dashboard, and the Operations Copilot.
- Complete the Data Classification and Ticket Handling training.
- Shadow two support shifts with your onboarding buddy.

## Week 2
- Handle P4 tickets with your buddy reviewing your replies.
- Read the Customer Escalation Process and the Incident Communication Guidelines.

## Week 4
- Start handling P3 tickets independently.
- Join the weekly ticket queue review.

## Day 30
Meet with your manager for a 30-day check-in. Your manager confirms whether you are ready to join the P2 rotation.
