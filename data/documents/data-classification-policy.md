# Data Classification Policy

## Classification levels
| Level | Examples | Who can access |
|---|---|---|
| Public | Marketing pages, published documentation | Anyone |
| Internal | Process guides, team notes, product FAQ | All employees |
| Confidential | Pricing approvals, performance reviews, headcount plans | Named roles only |
| Restricted | Credentials, security incident details, access-control rules | Named individuals or admins only |

## Handling rules
- Label documents with their classification when you create them.
- Share Confidential and Restricted information only with people who are authorized to see it.
- Never paste Restricted information into external tools, including AI assistants, without written approval from Security.

## Reporting mistakes
If you share information with someone who should not see it, report it to Security within 24 hours. Reporting a mistake is never a disciplinary issue on its own.
