# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

## Office attendance
Office-based employees work from the office at least 2 days per week. Fully remote employees are identified in their offer letter.

## Core hours
Everyone is available for meetings between 10:00 and 15:00 in their local time zone.

## Equipment
New employees receive a one-time $500 stipend for home office equipment. Company laptops must not be shared with anyone outside Corvane Systems.

## Security
Use the company VPN for all work on public or shared networks. Lock your screen whenever you step away.

## Working abroad
Employees may work from another country for up to 20 days per year with manager approval and a confirmation from People Operations.
