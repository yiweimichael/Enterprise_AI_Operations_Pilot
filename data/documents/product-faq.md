# Corvane Flow Product FAQ

## What is Corvane Flow?
Corvane Flow is a workflow automation platform for finance and operations teams. Customers use it to automate approvals, invoice processing, and recurring reports.

## Which plans are available?
There are three plans: Starter, Business, and Enterprise. Single sign-on (SAML) is included in the Business and Enterprise plans.

## What uptime do you guarantee?
The uptime commitment is 99.9% per month for the Business plan and 99.95% per month for the Enterprise plan. The Starter plan has no uptime commitment.

## Where is customer data stored?
Customers choose the US or EU data region when their account is created. Data does not move between regions.

## What are the API rate limits?
Business accounts can make 600 API requests per minute. Enterprise accounts can make 3,000 API requests per minute.

## How long are logs kept?
Workflow run logs are kept for 13 months.
