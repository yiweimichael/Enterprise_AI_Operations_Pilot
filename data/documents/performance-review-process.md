# Performance Review Process

Classification: Confidential.

## Review cycle
Reviews happen twice a year, in April and October. Each review covers the previous six months.

## Ratings
Managers rate each employee on a scale from 1 (does not meet expectations) to 5 (far exceeds expectations).

## Calibration
Managers in the same department meet to compare ratings before they are shared, so that the same standard is applied across teams.

## Promotions
Promotion nominations are submitted during the October cycle and need approval from the department director.

## Confidentiality
Ratings and calibration notes are visible only to the employee, their manager, and People Operations.
