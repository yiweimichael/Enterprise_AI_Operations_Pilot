# Travel and Expense Policy

## Scope
This policy applies to all Corvane Systems employees who travel or spend money on company business.

## Booking travel
Book flights and hotels through the company travel portal at least 14 days before departure when possible. Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or longer.

## Daily limits
- Hotel: up to $250 per night in major cities and $180 per night elsewhere.
- Meals: up to $75 per day, including tips.
- Ground transport: use public transit or ride-share. Rental cars need manager approval.

## Submitting expenses
Submit expenses within 30 days of the expense date, with itemized receipts for any item over $25. Expenses submitted more than 90 days after the expense date are not reimbursed.

## Approval
Your direct manager approves expense reports up to $2,500. Reports above $2,500 also need Finance approval.
