# Customer Escalation Process

## When to escalate
Escalate a ticket when any of the following is true:
- It is a P1 ticket.
- A P2 ticket is not resolved within 4 hours.
- The customer asks for executive contact.
- The customer mentions legal action or a possible data exposure.

## Escalation levels
1. **L1 - Support Engineer:** owns the ticket and gathers details.
2. **L2 - Operations Lead:** coordinates engineering help and customer updates.
3. **L3 - Director of Customer Operations:** paged automatically when a P1 ticket is open for more than 2 hours.

## How to escalate
Add the "escalated" tag to the ticket, write the reason in the ticket notes, and post a link in the #ops-escalations channel. During business hours, the Operations Lead acknowledges within 15 minutes.

## De-escalation
Only the Operations Lead who accepted the escalation can remove the "escalated" tag, after confirming the customer agrees the issue is under control.
