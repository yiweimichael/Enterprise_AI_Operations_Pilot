# Customer Retention Playbook

Classification: Confidential.

## Risk signals
Treat an account as at risk when any of the following is true:
- Active usage drops by more than 30% over 60 days.
- The account opens three or more P1 or P2 tickets in one quarter.
- The customer's main sponsor leaves their company.

## Actions for at-risk accounts
1. Schedule a call between the customer and an executive sponsor within 10 business days.
2. Agree on a written account health plan with clear owners and dates.
3. If needed, offer a retention discount within the limits of the Discount Approval Matrix.

## Target
The team's target is 92% gross revenue retention for the fiscal year.
