# Discount Approval Matrix

Classification: Confidential.

## New business discounts
| Discount on list price | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to 10% | Account Executive |
| More than 10% and up to 20% | Sales Manager |
| More than 20% and up to 30% | VP of Sales |
| More than 30% | CFO |

## Renewal discounts
The Customer Operations Manager can approve a retention discount of up to 15% for an at-risk customer at renewal. Retention discounts above 15% follow the new business table.

## Multi-year agreements
A multi-year agreement can receive an additional 5% discount on top of the approved discount.

## Who may offer discounts
Operations analysts may not promise or discuss discounts with customers. Send discount requests to the account owner.
