# Incident Communication Guidelines

## First update
Post the first status page update within 20 minutes of a P1 incident being declared, even if the cause is unknown.

## Update frequency
Update the status page at least every 30 minutes until the incident is resolved, and say when the next update will come.

## Writing style
- Use plain language and describe the customer impact first.
- Do not speculate about the root cause.
- Do not name individual customers or employees.

## After the incident
Send affected customers a summary of the cause and the follow-up actions within 5 business days of resolution.
