# Operations Headcount Plan FY2027

Classification: Confidential.

## Current team
The Operations team has 42 employees across support engineering, operations analysis, and team leadership.

## Planned hires
The plan is to grow to 50 employees by the end of Q4 FY2027:
- 5 support engineers based in the EU, to cover EU business hours.
- 2 operations analysts.
- 1 data analyst for reporting and forecasting.

## Hiring rules
Hiring is paused for any role whose quarterly budget is more than 5% over plan. Backfills for departing employees do not need extra approval.
