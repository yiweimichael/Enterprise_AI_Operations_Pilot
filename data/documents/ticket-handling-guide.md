# Ticket Handling Guide

## Purpose
This guide explains how the Operations team handles customer tickets in the CRM.

## Priority levels and response targets
| Priority | Definition | First response | Resolution target |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Production outage affecting all users at a customer | 30 minutes | 4 hours |
| P2 | Major feature unavailable with no workaround | 2 hours | 1 business day |
| P3 | Feature degraded but a workaround exists | 1 business day | 5 business days |
| P4 | Question or minor issue | 2 business days | 10 business days |

## Ticket statuses
Tickets move through these statuses: new, open, pending_customer, resolved, closed. A resolved ticket closes automatically after 7 days without a customer reply.

## Required fields
Every ticket needs a customer account ID, a priority, a one-sentence summary, and an owner. The shift lead reassigns any ticket that has no owner for more than 1 hour.

## Updating tickets
Record every customer contact in the ticket. Changing a ticket's priority requires a note explaining why. Tickets are never deleted; close them with a reason instead.

Changes proposed by the Operations Copilot are applied only after a human approves them.
