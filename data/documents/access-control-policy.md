# Access Control Policy

Classification: Restricted.

## Least privilege
Employees get only the access their job requires. Access is granted by role, never by copying another employee's permissions.

## Operations Copilot roles
| Role | Can read | Can request CRM changes | Can approve CRM changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| analyst | Internal documents | Yes | No |
| manager | Internal and Confidential documents | Yes | Yes, but not their own requests |
| admin | All documents | Yes | Yes, but not their own requests |

## Access reviews
Managers review their team's access every quarter. Access that is not confirmed within 10 business days of the review is removed.

## Leavers
All access is removed within 24 hours of an employee's last working day.

## Emergency access
Break-glass admin accounts may be used only during a SEV1 incident, and every use is reviewed by Security.
