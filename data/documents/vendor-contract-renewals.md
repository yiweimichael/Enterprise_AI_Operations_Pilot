## Renewal process
Start every renewal review at least 30 days before its notice period ends. The owner compares usage against the contract and recommends renewing, renegotiating, or cancelling.

## Approval
Renewals under $50,000 per year are approved by the owning team's director. Renewals of $50,000 or more also need CFO approval.
